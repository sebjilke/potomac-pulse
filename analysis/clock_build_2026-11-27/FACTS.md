(2) The two helpers `getPoRtoGFTravelTime` and `getGFtoLFTravelTime` are separate functions with separate baselines (19.4 and 6.5, `shared/model.js:83-84`: `TRAVEL_POR_GF_BASELINE`, `TRAVEL_GF_LF_BASELINE`) but identical bodies and the same rise-rate reduction `Math.min(0.30, ratePerHour * 0.02)` (`shared/model.js:385-407`), and both take `mult`. (3) `getFlowMultiplier` is shared by every use (`model.js:367-371`). So editing `getGFtoLFTravelTime` or `TRAVEL_GF_LF_BASELINE` cannot change the PoR to GF time; editing `getFlowMultiplier`, `mult`, `TRAVEL_COEF`, `TRAVEL_EXP`, `MEDIAN_TRAVEL` or the rise-rate reduction constants can change both. Per the Nothing-else check in clock-study FACTS section 1, nothing after `:972` reads `mult`.
