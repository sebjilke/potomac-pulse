**3. MINOR: keyboard users cannot dismiss the bubble, and `aria-expanded` disagrees with what is on screen.**
- Evidence: `main.css` shows the bubble for `.col-info:focus-visible::after` as well as `.col-info.open::after`.
  - Tabbing onto an icon shows the bubble.
  - Enter or Space then sets `open` / `aria-expanded="true"` with no visible change.
  - A second Enter, or Escape, removes `open` and sets `aria-expanded="false"`, but the bubble stays visible for as long as the button keeps focus.
- Fix: drop the `:focus-visible::after` selector. Keyboard users can still open it with Enter or Space, since the click handler fires, and Escape then works. Alternatively use `:focus-visible:not(.dismissed)`, but the simpler fix is enough. Then update the CSS assertion in `test/map-removed.test.mjs` (the "bubble for .open and :focus-visible" test).
