### Item 6: Great Falls to Little Falls line under the header travel time

**What `#multVal` shows today.**
- Written only at `gauges-ui.js:158-163`, in `updateUI()`: `` `~${Math.round(mult.travelHrs)} hrs` ``.
- `mult = data._mult`, set by `calcTravelTimes()` (`src/data/fetch.js:143-147`) as `getFlowMultiplier(data[LF].q || MEDIAN_FLOW)`.
- `travelHrs = 4139 * max(LFflow, 1000)^-0.5963`, in `src/model/shared-model.js`.
- It is a **whole-reach Point of Rocks to Little Falls time at the current Little Falls flow**. It has no rise-rate reduction and no per-gauge iteration.
- It is only written when `mult` is truthy. The element is never reset, so it holds the old value through any later failure.
- It starts as `--` in `index.html:85`.
- It is rounded to a whole number with `~` and ` hrs`.

**How it relates to the model's two legs.**
- `TRAVEL_POR_GF_BASELINE` 19.4 plus `TRAVEL_GF_LF_BASELINE` 6.5 is 25.9, and `MEDIAN_TRAVEL` is 25.8. With the same multiplier the two legs sum to 1.004 times the header value. A rounding off-by-one is possible.
- Example at LF 4,940 cfs: header 25.96, PoR to GF 19.52, GF to LF 6.54.

**The model's own GF to LF value.**
- `gfEstimate.inputs.travelGFtoLF` is `getGFtoLFTravelTime(mult, riseRate)` at `great-falls.js:423`. It is shown at `great-falls-ui.js:266` as `toFixed(1) + " hrs"`.
- It can differ from the header in two ways:
  1. `mult` is reassigned inside the PoR to GF convergence loop (`great-falls.js:407-411`) to the multiplier at the **historic Point of Rocks flow**, not the current Little Falls flow.
  2. It applies the rising-river reduction, capped at 30%.
- **Concrete example:** LF 12,000 cfs and a rising river whose Point of Rocks flow 20h ago was 6,000 cfs.
  - Header: round(15.29) gives `~15 hrs`.
  - Header basis for GF to LF, `6.5 × mult(12,000)`, gives 3.85.
  - Panel basis, `mult(6,000)`, gives 5.82, or about 5.6 after a 4% rise reduction.
  - Panel row: "5.6 hrs". A line computed from the header basis would read `~4 hrs`, contradicting the panel.

**Recommendation.** Source = `gfEstimate.inputs.travelGFtoLF`.
- It is the model's own current value, it needs no new calculation, and it cannot contradict the inputs-panel row, which reads the same number.
- Format it as the header does: `~${Math.round(x)} hrs`. Rounding the panel value keeps the two consistent. For example, 5.6 shows as "5.6 hrs" in the panel and "~6 hrs" in the header line.
- The other defensible choice is `getGFtoLFTravelTime(data._mult.mult, null)`. It shares the header's flow basis exactly, but it is a second calculation and can disagree with the panel. This is open question 1. The plan assumes the recommended source.
