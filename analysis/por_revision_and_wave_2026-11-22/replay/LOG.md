## Anomalies and facts met
1. **Readings arrive late in bursts.** An 8-hour pattern: at the 04:00, 12:00 and 20:00 UTC runs the stored history holds fewer stamps than the record shows today (`in.porHist.n` 852 against 853 to 862 if complete); the missing stamps carry `last_modified` about 2 minutes after the run began and appear in the next run's history. This is why attempt 2 (availability by `last_modified`) was needed. For 13 Nov and earlier (the old world) no publication stamp exists; eight Gate B runs keep this count mismatch (seq 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64) with no effect on `hist.*`, `rise.*` or anything after them.
2. **Values rewritten after a run.** seq 212 (18 Nov 13:00): the 12:00 Point of Rocks reading was logged 1840 cfs at 1.13 ft; the record now has 1860 cfs at 1.14 ft (`last_modified` 13:03:30); the job stored 1840 first-seen. Whole run differs, and seq 217's rise rate through the stored stamp. Water temperature rewritten by 0.1 degC at seq 56, 136, 147.
3. **Old-reading runs are a prefix.** Classifying "old" by "logged flow differs from the current record" also catches seq 212 (rewritten, see 2); the builder therefore defines the old runs as the contiguous prefix up to the first run whose logged flow equals the current record (CURVE_DEFINITION rule 1 and `f_old/build_f_old.py`).
4. **Archive timestamp anomaly of the data builder** (tools/LOG.md anomaly 1, old files starting an hour early in daylight months) was not touched; the replay uses archive values only for stamps up to 9 Nov 23:55, for which `tools/04_time_alignment_check` found zero shift.
5. Old-curve diagnostic (in `F_OLD_REPORT.md`, not used by the curve): archive pairs at 1.01 and 1.02 ft hold one flow each (1200 and 1220 cfs, latest 11 Oct), beside rule A's 1190 and 1210. Seen, not investigated.
6. The `f_old` test readings all sit at levels that are knot levels: the test shows the lookup kept its values over the days; it does not test interpolation between knots (stated in the report).

- Old curve, written before the test (`f_old/CURVE_DEFINITION.md`): "the last old days" = pairs from 7 Nov 00:00 to the last old pair; latest pair per level wins; linear interpolation rounded to 10 cfs; rule A extends the two end knots linearly, rule B scales the current record by the ratio at the two end knots. The brief left the definition to the builder.
- Gate A uses 4 reconstruction facts that are not in the brief's text: (a) the availability rule by `last_modified` (attempt 2); (b) the revision boundary read from the logged current flow; (c) the pre-log history is reconstructed as the frozen readings in `(clock - 72 h, qT]` of the first run; (d) bins before the first snapshot (11 Nov 03:00) are that snapshot, justified by the first check being due on 11 Nov 09:07.
- The temperature field is a counted gate field in my spec (the brief's list says "the current readings themselves"); two of the four Gate A misses are temperature differences of 0.1 degC. The spec was not changed after seeing this.
- `final.ts` is used as the frozen clock for the history trimming as well (production trims at the run's own `Date.now()`, 1 to 3 s earlier); the trimming cutoff is at least 52 s away from any 5-minute stamp on every run.
- Gate B in-sample caveat: for old-reading runs the current-reading comparison is circular.
- The loader appends one export line in memory to `scheduled-update.js` (file on disk unchanged) to reach `storePoRHistory`.
- T2: validation moment of a run is taken as its `final.ts`; one due time lies within 3 s of a run clock (`t2_ambiguous_within_3s`, count in `out/facts.json` after a ruling).
- The R8a sample "replay of a sample of runs under each version" is covered by replaying all runs with each run's own version (v39.2 2 runs, v39.6 164, v39.11 114); the break test at the switch is for the analysts.
