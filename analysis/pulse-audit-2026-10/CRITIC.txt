2. GAPS INVESTIGATED
=====================================================================

G1. The service worker serves stale USGS data labelled as fresh. HIGH. Single-source: the mechanism is read from code and the effect is simulated.

Code (frozen v37.21; the service worker shipped in v37.10 on 06-23, so it was live during the flood):
- vite.config.js sets StaleWhileRevalidate on `waterservices.usgs.gov|corsproxy|allorigins|api.water.noaa.gov` (cache pp-data, maxAge 6 h).
- fetch.js:386 uses a constant URL (period=P7D), so every fetch hits the same cache key.
- When a cached entry exists, the service worker returns it and refreshes the cache in the background.
- fetchUSGS then calls setLastFetchTime(Date.now()) and sets dataSource "live" (fetch.js:398-399).
- updateStalenessDisplay (fetch.js:544-578) shows "Updated just now" from the fetch time, not the USGS reading dateTime.

Effects:
- A returning visitor first sees data from their previous visit, up to 6 h old, labelled fresh, for up to 15 min (the refresh interval).
- After that, every render is one fetch cycle (15 min) behind, on top of USGS latency.
- The sync-learning bins and advisories are cached the same way (pp-api, 24 h TTL). The advisory freshness guards (2 h) partly protect the banners.
- TODO #19 itself lists the in-browser check of this behaviour as a "pending manual gap".

Effect size, with the server replay standing in for the browser (c1_sw_staleness.txt):
- LF 6 h stale vs current LF: onset mean −20.5% (worst −62%), rise −11.0% (worst −30.8%), peak −12.4% (worst −31.2%). The largest deficit was −7,000 cfs at 10-14T10.
- Displayed estimate (approx-corrected) rendered 6 h late vs truth for tick t:
  - onset MAPE 50.8 → 66.6
  - rise 11.4 → 19.0 (bias −7.0 → −17.1)
  - peak 3.9 → 14.4
  - recession 8.7 → 6.9 (stale happens to help on the fall)
- 1 h stale: onset worst shift −27%. 15 min: up to −5.8% on LF at onset.
- The 120% ceiling is computed client-side from the same stale LF, so on the rise the cap was stale too.

How many paddlers saw stale data is not observable: no browser telemetry exists.

Recommendation:
- Switch the USGS and NWS routes to NetworkFirst with a timeout, or add a cache-buster.
- Drive "Updated …" from the newest reading dateTime.
- Set dataSource from the response's actual freshness.

G2. The Creeks tab has no upper or flood threshold. MEDIUM, safety wording. Single-source.

Code:
- CREEK_RUNS (constants.js:114-119) defines only `runnable`.
- creeks-ui.js:263-264 shows "Running! (≥N cfs)" with a green glow, and the banner says "CREEKS ARE RUNNING", for any q ≥ runnable.

During the flash-flood peak, the tab told paddlers these Class III-V creeks were "Running!" in green, with no high-water warning. Whether 8× is above the safe upper limit for Difficult Run is not checked here; American Whitewater ranges are outside the allowlist.

G3. Most of the onset "local runoff" is Difficult Run, which the app already fetches. HIGH for interpreting accuracy. Single-source, but it explains a verified number.

Difficult Run (01646000) joins the Potomac between Great Falls and Little Falls. Its flow is therefore inside the LF "truth" but can never be in a GF estimate.

Comparison with the verified H5 residual (c3_difficult_run.txt):

| Time | H5 residual | Difficult Run excess over baseline |
|---|---|---|
| 10-11T15Z | +756 | +793 |
| 10-11T16Z | +1,638 | +1,171 |
| 10-11T17Z | +2,201 | +1,431 |
| 10-11T18Z | +2,110 | +1,561 |
| 10-11T19Z | +2,193 | +1,581 |
| 10-11T20Z | +2,204 | +1,481 |
| 10-11T21Z | +1,566 | +1,291 |
| 10-11T22Z | +759 | +1,011 |

So Difficult Run alone accounts for about 65-100% of the "unexplained" residual.

At the onset ticks' validationDue:
- Difficult Run averaged 686 cfs, 12.0% of mean truth (max 1,590).
- Scoring against LF minus Difficult Run moves raw onset MAPE from 46.0% to 39.4% and bias from −31.1% to −21.9%.

Consequences:
- The app scores and learns Great Falls error against inflow that enters below Great Falls.
- That inflow was measured and fetched (creeks tab), but never subtracted.
- It contaminates every validation during local storms, the low-flow bins included.

Recommendation: subtract Difficult Run (and an ungauged-area scaling) from the LF truth at validation, or at least flag validations where Difficult Run is above some fraction of LF.

3. CONTRADICTIONS AND VERDICTS THAT LOOK TOO GENEROUS OR TOO HARSH
=====================================================================

C1. Onset cause.
- Positions held:
  - The replay notes say "dominated by local runoff".
  - E5 (verified CONFIRMED) says "mostly unforecastable local runoff".
  - H5 (CORRECTED) says gauged-tributary timing dominates, with about 2k cfs of ungauged runoff for about 6 h.
  - D3-verify says the worst onset misses were "local runoff".
- G3 shows the "ungauged" part is mostly Difficult Run, which is gauged.
- E5's CONFIRMED is too generous. The onset error mixes horizon (E5 itself: 46.0 → 29.8 at +3 h), tributary timing (H5/H6), and below-GF inflow that is measurable (G3). It should read CORRECTED.

C2. Rising-limb raw fidelity.
- The replay builder says the gate passes 20/20.
- L6 (verified CONFIRMED) shows a failed hard constraint: the n=1 bin (sumErrorSq = 9,743²) forces raw = 20,743 at the 10-12T15 tick. The replay has 16,401 (−20.9%) at every lag setting.
- At that tick the travel time was 45.4 h, the maximum (OPS-08), and the iteration had not converged. That is exactly where a different first-seen PoR history would swing the lookback. This is a plausible cause, not a tested one.
- E1, E2, D3 and the replay "Rise" row (23.4% / +23.2%) were verified as reproductions of the replay, not as true production raw. Under amendment 18, every rising-limb raw magnitude should carry an UNVERIFIABLE label, and the E1 verdict omits that label.

C3. Ceiling binding: raw vs corrected.
- E2-verify additional finding 2 says "the gf_history line on the rise was mostly 1.2×LF (46/54)". L2's 62/72 is the same raw-binding measure.
- OPS-03-verify shows 46/54 is raw binding. Corrected (displayed) binding with the 10-15 bins is 27/54, and true production binding is not observable hourly.
- E2's extra claim is too strong. Only the 8 stored rows are proven clipped.

C4. Advisory timing.
- L3 says the banner was "12 h late". L7 and D4-verify show it was already up 10-11T12 to 10-12T00 for the wrong reason.
- The verifiers caught this, but L3 should be cited only in its corrected form: about 2.5 h of real gain, plus removing a 2-h lapse.

C5. CI mechanism.
- D1 blames the tiny learned bin SD. D1-verify shows the band uses only the fixed backtest q05/q95; stdDev is never rendered.
- The verifier is right. Drop D1's SD recommendation.

C6. Recession bias attribution. E6, L1 and D1/D5 each treat the recession under-read as having one cause. It has two stacked causes:
- The PoR-delta pushes raw down: raw −4.3%, and without the delta −0.6% (E6).
- The frozen +401 bin subtracts on top of that: corrected −9.1% (L1, exact bins).
E6-verify rightly rejected "the delta feeds the corrected miss". The report should present raw recession bias as delta-driven and the extra about −5 pp as bin-driven.

C7. D6 inconsistency left open.
- scheduled-update.js:898 stores predictedStage as the corrected stage.
- Yet 21/21 failure-row stages equal the raw stage.
- D6-verify noted this but marked CONFIRMED with "holds for this file". It is an unexplained export/storage discrepancy and should be an open OPS item, not closed.

C8. E3 vs H8.
- E3 is CONFIRMED with "missing attenuation" as the PoR-route cause.
- H8-verify adds water-supply withdrawals and rating bias as equal candidates.
- E3's recommendation (an attenuation term) rests on an inseparable attribution. CONFIRMED is fair on the numbers but generous on the cause.

C9. Display relevance of the peak over-read.
- E3/E4 rank the +31-37% peak raw over-read as high.
- The displayed values on those rows were ceiling-clipped: 20,880 displayed vs 20,937 truth.
- For users the peak over-read was mostly invisible. It mattered for learning, via the 25000-50000 and 12000-25000 rising bins (L2, L6). Severity for display is too high.

C10. Forecast persistence cross-check (F2). The verifier was correct and the analyst's reproduction was an artifact. No further issue.

C11. Network allowlist.
- The forecast analyst made GETs to api.water.noaa.gov, outside the allowlist (F9; flagged by the verifier).
- This is a process breach that should be stated in the final report.

V = verified by an independent verifier; S = single-source (analyst only, or critic only).

1. Recession under-read for about 3 days, and every safety layer missed it.
   - Two causes stacked: the PoR-delta pushed raw down, and Check 5 froze a wrong-sign bin (6000-12000/falling, +401) that kept subtracting from an already-low estimate. Corrected MAPE was 9.2% vs raw 5.6%, and the correction helped on 0 of 12 rows.
   - The 90% band missed 14 consecutive validations.
   - The residual banner never fired: worst was −13.8%, against a −15% latch with no persistence rule.
   - The headline accuracy barely moved: 7.379 → 7.3755.
   - Support: L1, E6, D1, D5, L8, L10, OPS-verify#6. [V]

2. A single pending slot plus a 13-16 h GF→LF horizon left the onset nearly unscored, so learning and advisories were structurally about 24 h late.
   - 1 of 26 onset predictions was scored.
   - The first flood pair arrived at 10-12T02. The first learned observation was at 10-12T15.
   - Support: L3 (corrected), D4, E5, H1/additional finding 4, PLAN amendment 13. [V]

3. Rising-limb travel/lookback mechanism.
   - The multiplier is taken from the lagged base-flow PoR, giving 48-61 h of travel against about 13 h observed.
   - The PoR-delta then scales the tributaries too.
   - Support: H1, E1, OPS-08 (non-convergence 22/54). [V for the mechanism]
   - Raw magnitudes are UNVERIFIABLE and may be biased low (C2, L6).

4. The 120%-of-LF ceiling on a stale LF value was effectively what the app showed on the rise.
   - It is never shown to the user.
   - Masked rows still trained the bins: 12000-25000/rising reached +3,762.
   - The -21.6% miss came from a cap fixed at creation and validated 13 h later.
   - Support: E2, D3 (corrected), L2 (corrected), OPS-03 (corrected). [V]
